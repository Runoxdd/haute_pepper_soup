# Haute Pepper Soup — Owner's Guide

**Welcome!** This guide is your go-to manual for running Haute Pepper Soup's website. Everything here is written for you — no tech background needed. If you can use WhatsApp and Instagram, you can do all of this.

Keep this guide saved somewhere handy (maybe as a note on your phone). You'll want to come back to it as you get comfortable with the admin panel.

---



## 1. Getting Started

### Opening Your Admin Panel

Your admin panel is where you manage your menu and orders. Think of it like the "back office" of your website — customers never see it, only you do.

1. Open your phone browser (Chrome, Safari, or whatever you normally use)
2. Type in your website address followed by `/admin` — for example: `hautepeppersoup.com/admin`
3. You'll see a "Sign in with Google" button — tap it
4. Sign in with the Google account you registered as the owner (the one your developer set up for you)
5. You're in! You'll see your dashboard

> **Tip:** Bookmark this page on your phone's home screen so you can tap it like an app. In Chrome, tap the three dots at the top right, then tap "Add to Home screen."

### First Time? Add Your First Dishes

If this is your very first time, the admin panel will walk you through adding your first dish. It's a friendly step-by-step wizard — just follow the prompts. You'll enter a dish name, description, price, and upload a photo.

Don't worry about getting everything perfect the first time. You can always edit everything later.

---

## 2. Managing Your Menu

This is where you'll spend most of your time in the admin panel. You can add new dishes, change prices, swap photos, and control what customers see.

### Adding a New Dish

1. Go to your admin panel (`yoursite.com/admin`)
2. Tap **"Edit Menu"** (or go to `yoursite.com/admin/menu`)
3. Tap **"Add New Dish"**
4. Fill in the details:
   - **Name** — e.g., "Goat Pepper Soup"
   - **Description** — A short, appetising description. E.g., "Slow-cooked goat meat in a fiery, aromatic broth with secret spices. Served hot."
   - **Price** — Enter the amount in Naira. Just the number, no need to type the ₦ sign. E.g., type `5000` for ₦5,000
   - **Category** — e.g., "Pepper Soup" or "Sides"
   - **Photo** — Upload a photo from your phone (more on this below)
   - **Side Options** — What sides can customers choose? E.g., "Boiled Yam", "Plantain", "None"
5. Tap **"Save"**
6. Your new dish is now live on the website!

### Editing an Existing Dish

Need to change a price or fix a typo? Easy.

1. Go to your admin menu page (`yoursite.com/admin/menu`)
2. Find the dish you want to change
3. Tap on it to expand the editing options
4. Change whatever you need — price, description, name, sides
5. Tap **"Save"**

Changes show up on your website straight away.

### Uploading a Photo for a Dish

Great photos sell food. Here's how to upload one:

1. When adding or editing a dish, tap the **photo upload area**
2. Your phone will ask if you want to take a photo or choose from your gallery — pick whichever you prefer
3. Select your photo
4. You'll see a preview before it saves — make sure it looks good!
5. Tap **"Save"**

**Important things about photos:**

- The photo must be under 2MB in size. Most phone photos are fine, but if yours is too large, the system will let you know
- Use JPEG, PNG, or WebP format (these are the standard formats your phone camera uses, so you don't need to worry about this)
- If a photo won't upload, try taking a new one — sometimes screenshots or heavily edited images cause issues

**Tips for taking great food photos:**
- Use natural light (near a window is perfect — avoid harsh overhead lights)
- Use a clean, simple background (a wooden table or plain plate works great)
- Get close to the food — fill the frame with the dish
- Make sure the food looks fresh and steam is visible if it's a hot dish
- Wipe any spills or drips from the plate edges

### Removing a Dish from the Menu

Maybe a dish is out of season, or you want to try something new.

1. Go to your admin menu page
2. Find the dish you want to remove
3. Tap **"Deactivate"** (or the toggle to turn it off)
4. Confirm when asked

**Good news:** The dish isn't actually deleted — it's just hidden from customers. This means:
- Past orders that included this dish still show the correct name and price
- You can bring it back anytime by tapping **"Reactivate"**

### Adding or Changing Side Options

Each dish can have its own list of sides that customers choose from (like Boiled Yam, Plantain, or None).

1. Go to your admin menu page
2. Tap on the dish you want to update
3. Find the **"Sides"** section
4. Type a new side name and tap **"Add"** — or remove an existing one by tapping the X next to it
5. Tap **"Save"**

Customers will see the updated side options immediately.

## 3. Handling Orders

This is the heart of your business — when customers place orders, here's how you'll know and what to do.

### How You'll Be Notified

When a customer places an order, two things happen:

1. **You get an email** — An automatic email is sent to your inbox with the full order details (what they ordered, their name, phone number, address, and any notes)
2. **The customer can message you on WhatsApp** — After placing their order, they see a button to message you on WhatsApp. The message is pre-filled with their order number so you know who they are

> **Tip:** Keep your email notifications turned on and check your inbox regularly. The email arrives within seconds of the order being placed.

### Viewing Your Orders

1. Go to your admin panel (`yoursite.com/admin`)
2. Tap **"View Orders"** (or go to `yoursite.com/admin/orders`)
3. You'll see a list of all orders, newest first
4. Tap any order to expand it and see the full details — items, sides, quantities, address, phone number, and any delivery notes

### Understanding Order Statuses

Every order goes through three stages:

| Status | What It Means | What You Need to Do |
|--------|--------------|---------------------|
| **Pending** | A new order just came in | Reach out to the customer |
| **Contacted** | You've spoken to the customer | Arrange payment and delivery |
| **Completed** | The food has been delivered/picked up | Nothing — you're done! |

### Marking an Order as "Contacted"

After you've called, texted, or WhatsApp'd the customer:

1. Go to your orders page
2. Find the order
3. Tap **"Mark Contacted"**

This helps you keep track of which customers you've already reached out to, especially on busy days.

### Marking an Order as "Completed"

After the food has been delivered or picked up:

1. Go to your orders page
2. Find the order (it should already show "Contacted")
3. Tap **"Mark Completed"**

This moves it out of your active view so you can focus on new orders.

### What If You See "Unnotified Orders"?

Sometimes, the notification email might not send successfully (maybe there was a brief internet hiccup). If this happens, you'll see a red warning on your dashboard that says **"Unnotified Orders"**.

Here's what to do:

1. Tap the warning or go to your orders page
2. You'll see which orders didn't get an email notification
3. Tap **"Resend"** next to the affected order — this will try sending the email again
4. If you see a **"Resend All"** button, you can tap it to retry all failed notifications at once

Don't panic — the order is still saved. You just didn't get the email about it.

### Finding Old Orders

Looking for a past order? You've got tools for that:

- **Date filter** — Pick a date range to see orders from a specific period
- **Status tabs** — Filter by All, Pending, Contacted, or Completed
- **Search** — Type a customer's name, phone number, or order reference (like HP-7291) to find a specific order

## 4. Understanding the Customer Experience

It helps to know what your customers see, so you can answer their questions and guide them.

### What Customers See When They Visit

When someone goes to your website, they see:
- A beautiful landing page with your brand name and featured dishes
- A full menu page with photos, descriptions, and prices for every active dish
- A WhatsApp contact button (floating at the bottom of the screen)
- Your business hours and contact info in the footer

### How They Place an Order

Here's the step-by-step from the customer's side:

1. **Browse the menu** — They scroll through your dishes, reading descriptions and seeing photos
2. **Add to cart** — They tap "Add to Order" on any dish they want. They choose a side option (like Plantain or Yam) and quantity
3. **Open their cart** — They tap the cart icon to review what they've selected. They can change quantities or remove items here
4. **Fill in their details** — They enter:
   - Name
   - Phone number
   - Delivery address
   - Any special notes (e.g., "Extra pepper please" or "Call me when you're close")
   - Whether they're interested in catering/party services (optional checkbox)
5. **Submit the order** — They tap "Place Order"
6. **See confirmation** — They get taken to a confirmation page with their order number (like **HP-7291**)

> **Note:** The customer's cart is saved on their phone even if they close the browser or lose connection. So if their internet drops while browsing, they won't lose what they've added.

### The Order Confirmation Page

After placing an order, the customer sees:
- Their unique order reference number (e.g., **HP-7291**)
- A summary of what they ordered and the total
- A big **"Contact us on WhatsApp"** button — tapping it opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message like: *"Hi, I placed order HP-7291 on Haute Pepper Soup"*
- They can save their reference number to check their order status later

### Guests vs. Signed-In Customers

- **Guests** (most customers) can place orders without creating an account. They just fill in their details and order. Simple.
- **Signed-in customers** (those who tap "Sign in with Google") get a bonus: they can see their past order history on the website.
- Both types of customers can always look up a specific order using their reference number.

You don't need to do anything differently — the system handles both automatically.

## 5. Your Dashboard

When you first open the admin panel, you land on your dashboard. Here's what everything means.

### The Numbers

| What You See | What It Means |
|-------------|--------------|
| **Today's Orders** | How many orders have come in today |
| **Pending** | Orders you haven't contacted the customer about yet — these need your attention! |
| **Revenue** | Total value of today's orders in Naira (e.g., ₦45,000) |

### Quick Actions

From the dashboard, you can quickly jump to:
- **View Orders** — See and manage all your orders
- **Edit Menu** — Add, change, or remove dishes

### The "Unnotified Orders" Warning

If you see a red banner at the top saying **"Unnotified Orders"**, it means one or more order notification emails failed to send. This doesn't mean the order is lost — it's still safely saved.

What to do:
1. Tap the warning
2. Tap **"Resend All"** to retry sending the emails
3. The warning disappears once all notifications are sent successfully

## 6. Tips for Success

Running a food delivery website is exciting! Here are some tips to help you make the most of it.

### Take Great Food Photos

Your photos are what sell your food online. Invest a few minutes in making them look appetising:
- Shoot near a window for soft, natural light
- Use a clean plate on a simple background
- Get close — let the food fill the screen
- Show textures (the broth, the steam, the meat falling off the bone)
- Take several photos and pick the best one

### Keep Your Prices Updated

If your ingredient costs change, update your prices right away. Customers trust a site with accurate pricing.

### Respond to Orders Quickly

Speed matters. Try to contact customers within **1 hour** of receiving their order, especially during lunch and dinner hours. Quick responses build trust and repeat customers.

### Share Your Website Link Everywhere

The more people know about your site, the more orders you'll get:

- **Instagram** — Put your website link in your bio. Share your menu in Stories with a "Link" sticker pointing to your site
- **WhatsApp Status** — Post your link regularly with a tempting photo of your food
- **WhatsApp Groups** — Share in relevant groups (family, friends, community groups)
- When someone asks about your menu on WhatsApp, send them the link instead of typing everything out — it's faster and looks more professional

### Use Your Order Reference Numbers

When a customer messages you, ask for their order number (the HP-XXXX code). This makes it easy to look up exactly what they ordered without any confusion.

## 7. Common Questions

**"How do I change my admin email?"**
Contact your developer — they'll update it for you. It takes less than 5 minutes on their end.

**"Can I add more than 3 dishes?"**
Yes! You can add as many dishes as you like. There's no limit.

**"What if I want to change the website colours or design?"**
Contact your developer. The design is customised for your brand, so any visual changes need their help.

**"How do customers pay?"**
There's no online payment on the website. When a customer places an order, you contact them to arrange payment however you prefer — bank transfer, cash on delivery, or any method that works for you both. You're in control.

**"Can I see how many people visit my website?"**
Yes! Your website has built-in visitor tracking. Ask your developer to show you — they can walk you through the numbers (total visitors, most popular pages, etc.).

**"What happens if I accidentally deactivate a dish?"**
No problem at all. Just go back to the menu page and tap "Reactivate" on that dish. It'll reappear on your website immediately.

**"What if a customer places an order but I can't fulfil it?"**
Contact them using the phone number or WhatsApp from the order details. Let them know as soon as possible and offer an alternative or reschedule. Then mark the order as "Contacted" so you remember you've handled it.

**"Can I use this on my phone?"**
Absolutely. The admin panel is designed to work perfectly on your phone. You don't need a laptop or computer.

**"What does the HP in the order number mean?"**
HP stands for **H**aute **P**epper — it's your brand's initials! Each order gets a unique code like HP-7291 so you and your customer can easily refer to it.
